HackerTwo - Features
Automated Qualification System - Evaluates user performance and determines eligibility for real penetration testing opportunities with partner companies.
White Hat Practice Environment - Provides a safe and legal platform where users can test cybersecurity skills without restrictions on dedicated training targets.

Attack and Defense Challenges - Allows users to launch and defend against simulated attacks from other players in a controlled environment.

Offline Mode - Enables users to practice hacking and penetration testing challenges without requiring an internet connection.

Multiplayer and Ranked Modes - Includes Casual Multiplayer for friendly competition and Ranked Mode for competitive skill-based progression and leaderboards.
